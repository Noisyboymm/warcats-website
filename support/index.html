Support

Help with signing in, connecting and playing.

Contact us

Email warcats.support@gmail.com. Tell us which device you play on and what happened, and include your player name. We read every message.

News and updates: Instagram, YouTube. The developer is on Instagram.

Common questions

I can't sign in

Use the same login you used last time: Epic, Google or Steam. All of them open the same profile once they are linked.

If you see "Your session has expired", sign in again. If the buttons are greyed out for a few seconds, wait; the game pauses sign-in after failed tries.

To add another login to your profile, open Profile on the main menu and use Linked logins.

The game lags or I can't connect to a match

Warcats matches run directly between players, not on a game server. Your ping and connection type show in the bottom-left corner during a match.

Try Wi-Fi closer to the router or a wired connection, close apps that use the network, and turn off a VPN. Restarting your router can also help with a strict connection type.

My game closed in the middle of a match

Open Warcats again within 15 minutes. If your match is still running, the game takes you back in. If it shows Match ended, the match is over.

If the player hosting the match drops out, the match moves to another player and carries on.

How do I report, mute or vote to kick a player?

Open the in-game menu and go to the players list. You can mute a player's voice or chat, report them, or start a vote to kick them.

How do I change my name?

Open Profile on the main menu and tap Change name. Names can only be changed once in a while, and some words are not allowed.

I bought gold bars but I did not get them

Close the game and open it again, then check your gold in the shop. If it is still missing, email us with your player name and the Google Play order number from your receipt. Google Play also lets you request a refund under its own rules.

I was penalised for leaving a ranked match

Leaving a ranked match, even by accident, triggers an automatic penalty. Open the game again within 15 minutes to rejoin a match that is still running. If you think a penalty or ban is wrong, email us.

Voice chat does not work on Android

Warcats needs the microphone permission. Open your phone's Settings, then Apps, then Warcats, then Permissions, and allow the microphone. Then open the game and join a match again.
