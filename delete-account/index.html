Delete your account

You can delete your account and its data in the game. If you cannot, contact us.

Delete your account in the game

  1. Open Warcats and sign in.
  2. On the main menu, tap Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE and tap Delete forever.

The game then closes. This cannot be undone.

You uninstalled the game

  1. Install Warcats again.
  2. Sign in with the same login you used before (Epic, Google or Steam).
  3. Follow the steps above.

Signing in proves the account is yours, so your data is deleted straight away.

You can't sign in any more

Email warcats.support@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Warcats account. Include your player name, your Player ID (the first 8 characters shown in Profile, if you remember it) and the login you used.

We will reply within 30 days and tell you what we can do.

What is deleted

  • Your player name and profile
  • Your friends list
  • Your rank (your leaderboard entry is reset to 0)
  • Your cat look and weapon skins
  • Your saved session and settings stored in the cloud

What we can't delete

Your Epic, Google or Steam account belongs to that company. Deleting your Warcats account does not delete it, and the data those companies hold is covered by their own policies.
