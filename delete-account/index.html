Delete your account

You can delete your account and its data in the game. If you cannot, contact us.

Last updated 26 November 2026.

Delete your account in the game

  1. Open Warcats and sign in.
  2. On the main menu, tap Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE and tap Delete forever.

The game then closes. This cannot be undone.

You uninstalled the game

  1. Install Warcats again.
  2. Sign in with the same login you used before (Epic, Google or Steam).
  3. Follow the steps above.

Signing in proves the account is yours, so your data is deleted straight away.

You can't sign in any more

Email warcats.support@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account. Include your player name, your Player ID (the first 8 characters shown in Profile, if you remember it) and the login you used (Epic, Google or Steam). We may ask you to prove the account is yours first.

We reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your player name, avatar and profile
  • Your friends list and your cat look
  • Your weapon skins
  • Your rank data (your leaderboard rank is reset to 0)
  • Your gold and credits. Gold bought with real money is not refunded, and anything unused is lost.
  • Your saved session and the copies of this data on your device

What is not deleted

  • Epic Online Services keeps an anonymous, empty account ID with no game data in it.
  • Other players' friends lists may still show your old friend code until they remove it. The code no longer links to any data.
  • Your Epic, Google or Steam account belongs to that company. Deleting your Warcats account does not delete it.
  • Google Play keeps its own purchase records. We may keep a purchase confirmation where the law requires us to keep accounting records.
