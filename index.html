How a round plays

Two teams of five. One bomb. Every round is short, and every round starts from zero positions.

The first team to win enough rounds takes the match. Teams swap sides halfway, and a tie goes to overtime.

  1. Buy

    Spend your credits on a gun, a shield and grenades before the round starts. Win rounds and you earn more.

  2. Fight for the map

    Five cats a side. Walls block sight, so every corner is a choice between peeking and waiting.

  3. Plant at A or B

    One team carries the bomb to a bomb site and plants it. The other team holds the sites.

  4. Defuse or detonate

    Once the bomb is down the clock is running. Defuse it in time, or it goes off.
