Privacy policy

What Warcats collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Effective 26 November 2026.

Who we are

Warcats ("we") makes the game Warcats and the website playwarcats.com. You can reach us at warcats.support@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

  • Sign-in. You sign in with Epic, Google or Steam through Epic Online Services. We receive a Product User ID (a random ID for your profile), and the display name from the login you used. With Google, we also receive your profile picture so we can show it as your avatar.
  • Your profile. Your player name, avatar, friends list, rank, cat look, weapon skins and the setting that you confirmed you are 13 or older. These are saved in the cloud storage of Epic Online Services so they follow you across devices.
  • Matches. Warcats matches run directly between players. During a match your device sends things like your position, shots and text chat to the other players in it. We run no game servers and do not keep match recordings.
  • Voice chat. If you use voice chat, your voice is sent to the players in your party or match while you play. We do not record it.
  • Cheat checks. The game checks for impossible play, such as movement that is too fast, and can flag a rank record that was changed.
  • Ads. The game can show ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and how you interact with ads.
  • On your device. Settings, the login you chose to remember, and short-lived information that lets you rejoin a match after a crash.

We use this to run the game: signing you in, matching you with players, saving your progress, keeping matches fair, and answering your messages.

Your IP address and other players

Because matches are peer to peer, the players in your match, and the relay services that help devices connect, can see your IP address. Only play with people you are comfortable with, and use a VPN if you want to hide your address.

Who else handles your data

  • Epic Games (Epic Online Services): sign-in, cloud storage, matchmaking and connecting players.
  • Google: Google sign-in, Google Play and Google AdMob.
  • Valve: Steam sign-in, if you use it.

We do not sell your data. These companies may process data in countries other than yours.

Children

Warcats is for people aged 13 and over. The game asks you to confirm your age the first time you play. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we delete it.

How long we keep it

We keep your profile until you delete your account. Rejoin information on your device expires after about 15 minutes.

Your choices

  • Delete your account and data in the game or by email. See how to delete your account.
  • Ask for a copy, or a correction, of the data we hold by emailing us. You may have other rights under the law where you live, and we will honour them.
  • Ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Voice and chat: you can mute any player, and report them, from the in-game players list.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change is important, we will tell you in the game.

Contact

Questions about privacy: warcats.support@gmail.com.
