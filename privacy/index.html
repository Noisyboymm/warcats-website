Privacy policy

What Warcats collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Last updated 26 November 2026. Published by Kuzone, India.

Who we are

Warcats is made by Kuzone, India ("we"). This policy covers the game Warcats and the website playwarcats.com. Contact: warcats.support@gmail.com.

Signing in

You sign in with Epic Games, Google or Steam. Sign-in is handled by Epic Online Services (EOS). Your login is linked to one Product User ID, a random ID for your profile. We never receive your password or your email address. We get the display name from the login you used, and with Google we also get your profile picture so we can show it as your avatar.

What we store, and why

These are saved in your EOS cloud storage so they follow you across devices:

  • Your player name, avatar and the setting that you confirmed you are 13 or older.
  • Your friends list.
  • Your cat look and the weapon skins you own or have equipped.
  • Your gold, credits, and the items you unlocked with them.
  • Your ranked data: rank, points, wins, losses and leaver penalties. If the game finds that this file was tampered with, it adds a review flag to it.

These stay on your device only: graphics and control settings, volume, the players you muted, the login you chose to remember, and short-lived information that lets you rejoin a match after a crash (it expires after about 15 minutes).

We use this to run the game: sign you in, match you with players, save your progress, keep ranked play fair and answer your messages.

Matches, chat and voice

  • Matches run directly between players. During a match your device sends your position, shots and similar data to the other players in it. We run no game servers and do not keep match recordings.
  • Text chat goes to the players in your match or lobby. We do not store it on a server.
  • Voice chat is live, goes to the players in your party or match, and is never recorded. The game asks for the microphone permission on Android before it uses the microphone.
  • Mute, hide and report. You can mute any player, hide chat and report players. Reports are sent through EOS.

Your IP address and other players

Because matches are peer to peer, the other players in your match can potentially see your network address. EOS can relay the connection to reduce this. Only play with people you are comfortable with, and use a VPN if you want to hide your address.

Fair play checks

The game checks other players' movement for impossible play and checks that rank files were not changed. Suspicious cases are logged on your device and may be sent to us as a report.

Ads

The game offers optional rewarded video ads. They appear only when you tap to watch one, never during a match. Ad providers such as Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address, approximate location and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it, for example in the EU and UK, the game asks for your consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases

You can buy gold bars through Google Play or your platform's store. We receive a confirmation of the purchase, the item and a purchase ID. We never receive your payment details. The store keeps its own records under its own policy.

Who else handles your data

  • Epic Games (Epic Online Services): sign-in, cloud storage, matchmaking, relays and reports.
  • Google: Google sign-in, Google Play and Google AdMob.
  • Valve: Steam sign-in, if you use it.

We do not sell your data and we do not build our own advertising profiles. These companies may process data in countries other than yours.

Children

Warcats is for people aged 13 and over. The game asks you to confirm your age the first time you play. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we delete it on request or when we find it.

How long we keep it

We keep your profile until you delete your account. When you delete it, we delete the data listed on the delete account page. EOS keeps only an anonymous, empty account ID with no game data. We may keep a purchase confirmation where the law requires us to keep accounting records.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data in the game (Profile, then Delete account) or by email. See how to delete your account.
  • Ask for a copy or a correction of your data by emailing us. We reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to object to or limit how we use your data, and we will honour it.
  • Voice and chat: mute any player or hide chat from the in-game players list.

Security

Your files are stored by EOS. We take care with your data, but no system is completely secure and we cannot promise absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change is important, we will tell you in the game. Playing after a change means you accept it.

Contact

Questions about privacy: warcats.support@gmail.com.
